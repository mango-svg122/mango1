2026 BaZi Predictions: Year of the Fire Snake

How the Energy of 丁巳 (Ding Si) Shapes Your Fortune This Year

🐍

Year of the Fire Snake

🔥 Fire February 17, 2026 — February 5, 2027

Heavenly Stem: Ding (丁) — Yin Fire | Earthly Branch: Si (巳) — Snake

The Chinese zodiac year 2026 runs from February 17, 2026 (Lunar New Year) through February 5, 2027. This is the year of the Fire Snake (丁巳) — a particularly significant year in the 60-year sexagenary cycle because it combines the transformative energy of Yin Fire with the strategic, mysterious nature of the Snake.

The Energy of Ding (丁) — Yin Fire

Ding Fire is the flame of a candle or lantern — steady, warm, and illuminating. Unlike the blazing power of Bing (Yang Fire), Ding Fire represents a gentler but persistent light that guides through darkness. In 2026, this energy brings:

  • Intuition and insight — A year favoring deep thinking, research, and spiritual pursuits
  • Warm relationships — Better connections with family, partners, and close friends
  • Creative expression — Art, music, writing, and aesthetic pursuits are favored
  • Refinement — A focus on improving quality over quantity in all endeavors

The Si (巳) — Snake Energy

The Snake (巳) is associated with wisdom, strategy, and transformation. Snakes shed their skin, symbolizing renewal and rebirth. The Si energy this year brings:

  • Strategic thinking — Plans made now tend to succeed through careful positioning
  • Hidden opportunities — Things revealed beneath the surface become valuable
  • Personal transformation — Old patterns shed, new growth emerges
  • Financial wisdom — Better returns from careful investment than bold speculation

2026 BaZi Forecast by Day Master

甲 Jia (Wood Yang) — Your most favorable year in a cycle

The Fire Snake provides exactly what Jia Wood needs — warmth and nurturing energy. This is a year of growth, recognition, and career advancement. Your creativity flows naturally, and authority figures are supportive. Lucky directions: East, Southeast. Lucky colors: Green, Blue.

Fire fuels your growth. This is an excellent year for artistic pursuits, publishing, and anything involving communication. Relationships deepen, and there may be unexpected sources of income. Lucky directions: East, South. Lucky colors: Green, Red.

You face an excess of fire energy in 2026. While your energy is high, be cautious about overcommitting, conflicts, or burnout. Practice patience and avoid power struggles. Focus on long-term planning rather than impulsive action. Lucky directions: South, Northwest. Lucky colors: Red, White.

己 Ji (Earth Yin) — A year of steady progress

Your earth energy harmonizes well with the Snake's earth branch. This is a stable year for career development and relationships. Academic pursuits, teaching, and knowledge-sharing are favored. Lucky directions: Central, Northeast. Lucky colors: Yellow, Green.

癸 Gui (Water Yin) — A year of hidden opportunities

Fire creates Metal, which generates Water — a favorable indirect cycle. Opportunities may not be obvious at first. Look for them in research, analysis, and behind-the-scenes work. Your intuition is particularly strong this year. Lucky directions: North, East. Lucky colors: Black, Green.
